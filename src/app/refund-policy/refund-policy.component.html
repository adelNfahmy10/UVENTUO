At Uvéntuo, we care about making sure you receive your fragrance order exactly as expected. We carefully check our products before shipping them.

02 / ORDER CANCELLATION

Cancel Your Order

You may request to cancel your order before it has been shipped.

  • Cancellation requests should be made as soon as possible after placing the order.
  • Once the order has been shipped, it may no longer be possible to cancel it.
  • To request a cancellation, please contact us using the contact information below.
03 / RETURNS

Returns

Since our products are perfumes and fragrances that are opened and used directly, we accept returns only in specific cases.

Returns may be accepted if the product arrives damaged, defective, or different from the product that was ordered.

If you receive a damaged or incorrect product, please contact us as soon as possible after receiving your order.

04 / DAMAGED OR INCORRECT ORDERS

05 / REFUNDS

Refunds

If your order cancellation or return is approved, the refund will be processed according to the original payment method.

  • Refunds are processed after the order has been reviewed and approved.
  • The time required for the refunded amount to appear may vary depending on your bank or payment provider.
  • We will provide you with any available update regarding the refund process.
06 / PRODUCT EXCHANGE

Product Exchange

If you receive a damaged or incorrect product, we may provide a replacement depending on product availability and the specific situation.

Please contact us before sending any product back. We will provide you with the appropriate instructions.
