At Uvéntuo, we work to make sure your order reaches you safely and as quickly as possible. This policy explains how we prepare, ship, and deliver your orders.

02 / ORDER PROCESSING

Preparing Your Order

Orders are prepared after the order has been confirmed and payment has been successfully completed.

  • Orders are usually prepared within 1–2 business days.
  • Once prepared, your order is handed over to the delivery company.
  • During sales, holidays, or periods of high demand, processing may take longer.
03 / DELIVERY TIME

When Will It Arrive?

Delivery time depends on your location and the delivery area.

01

Egypt

Orders within Egypt usually arrive within 2–5 business days after the order has been shipped.

02

Delivery Times May Vary

Actual delivery times may vary depending on the delivery company, your location, and circumstances outside our control.

04 / SHIPPING FEES

Shipping Costs

Shipping fees, if applicable, will be displayed during checkout before you confirm your purchase.

Shipping costs may vary depending on your delivery location and the size or number of products included in your order.

05 / ORDER TRACKING

Track Your Order

If tracking is available for your order, tracking information may be provided to you after your order has been shipped.

You can also contact us at any time if you need an update regarding your order.

06 / DELIVERY ADDRESS

Make Sure Your Details Are Correct

Please make sure that your delivery information is correct and complete when placing your order.

  • Full name
  • Phone number
  • Complete delivery address

Uvéntuo is not responsible for delays or failed deliveries caused by incorrect or incomplete delivery information provided by the customer.

07 / DELIVERY DELAYS

Unexpected Delays

In some cases, delivery may take longer than the estimated timeframe due to circumstances outside our control.

  • Weather conditions
  • Delivery company issues
  • Public holidays
  • High order volumes
  • Other unforeseen circumstances

If you experience any issue with your delivery, please contact us and we will do our best to help you follow up on your order.
